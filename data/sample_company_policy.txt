Acme Company Policy Handbook

Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks before the requested start date. Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of five days.

Remote Work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's approval. Employees must be available during their team's agreed core hours and must maintain a suitable, secure workspace.

Expenses
Business travel and pre-approved business expenses are reimbursed when receipts are submitted within 30 days. Meals are reimbursable up to $50 per day while travelling on approved business.

Information Security
Employees must use strong passwords and enable multi-factor authentication on company accounts. Company documents containing confidential information must not be shared through personal email accounts.
